Online polar alignment assistant for telescope mounts

Easy Polar Align helps you roughly polar align an equatorial telescope mount using your phone's compass, orientation sensors, and location. The polar alignment aid estimates the direction of the north or south celestial pole, including when Polaris is hidden.

Phone sensors and nearby metal can affect accuracy. Use a polar scope, plate solving, or another star-based method for precise alignment.
