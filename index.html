BEFORE YOU ALIGN02 / 02
01

Lay your phone flat. Point the top edge toward your mount’s polar axis.

02

Give it some space. Metal, magnets and electronics can throw off your compass. Calibrate with a figure-eight motion.

03

Fine-tune under the stars. This is a starting point, not a precision polar alignment. Set your mount’s altitude to your latitude, then use a polar scope or star-based alignment.

Compass settings +

Most phone compasses report magnetic north. If you know your browser provides a true-north-referenced heading, select true north to avoid correcting it twice.

Browser and device implementations differ. Verify the reference against a known bearing. Changing this setting does not calibrate the sensor.
